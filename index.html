I don’t want distraction. I don’t want more features.

I want simplicity. I want focus. I want to write.

Blank is a lean word processor set with the most essential features and nothing else.

Blank isn’t code-signed yet, so the first time you open it your system may warn that it’s from an unknown developer. It’s safe, here’s how to open it:

macOS: right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again.

Windows: if you see “Windows protected your PC,” click More info, then Run anyway.

Linux: extract the archive, then run ./blank (add --no-sandbox if it complains).

Pages: US Letter, numbered, with real page breaks.

Fonts: Times New Roman and eight more, sizes 8 to 72.

Styling: bold, italic, underline.

Alignment: left, center, right, justified.

Lists: bulleted and numbered, with indent in and out.

Line spacing: 1.0, 1.5 or 2.0.

Margins: narrow, normal or wide.

Zoom: zoom the page in and out.

Paste: arrives as plain text and takes the page’s formatting.

Right-click: undo, redo, cut, copy, paste, spelling suggestions.

Find: whole-word search, jump to each match by page.

Replace: hold a result to replace that one match.

Spell check: scans the document, lists misspellings by page, fix or ignore.

Go to page: jump straight to any page number.

Documents: keep many at once in the app, auto-saved, rename, reorder, search, delete.

Save as: .docx, PDF, EPUB, .rtf, .txt, .html, .json.

Open: .docx, .rtf, .txt, .html, .json.

Display: light and dark, plus a warm mode for the eyes.

Toolbar: hidden until you reach for it near the top.

Auto-save: your work is kept as you type.
